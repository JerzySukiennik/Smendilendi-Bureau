Every row is a sound the game can play. measured is master.gain.value × bus.gain.value × handle.gain.gain.value read off the real WebAudio nodes after actually playing it in this page — not a recomputation. manifest is master × bus × asset gain × context, computed straight from the two JSON files without touching engine code. reviewed is the number stored in review/batches.json, i.e. what the review page showed Jurek. Amber rows are the levels he changed by hand. A row marked dyn can only ever be quieter than the number shown: dynamic attenuates and never amplifies.
