## Processing

No source file is shipped as downloaded. Every file was: resampled to 44.1 kHz; downmixed to mono where it is positional (SFX, UI, radio, OS chimes) or kept stereo where it is not (music, ambience); loudness-matched (music/ambience/radio to ≈ −16 LUFS with two-pass linear `loudnorm`, one-shots to a matched peak-window RMS) and peak-limited below −1 dBFS; trimmed and wrap-crossfaded into a seamless loop where it loops; and encoded twice, to Vorbis q4 (`.ogg`) and AAC (`.m4a`, for Safari/iOS).

The twelve files replaced in the 2026-08-27 sign-off were rebuilt by `assets/audio/build/build-signoff.mjs`, which is in the repository and reproduces the same audio bit for bit (the Ogg container carries a random stream serial, so the file bytes differ between runs): silence trimmed at −55 dBFS on both ends, then two-pass linear `loudnorm` to −14 LUFS / −1.0 dBTP for anything with at least 400 ms of content (EBU R128 cannot measure less), or a static gain putting the loudest 50 ms window at −18 dBFS RMS for anything shorter, then `alimiter` at −2.0 dBFS. Two dB of headroom rather than one because both codecs overshoot on decode — an AAC of a signal limited to −1.0 dBFS came back at +1.03 dBFS — and the script closes the loop: it decodes what it just encoded and pulls the master down again if either the `.ogg` or the `.m4a` lands above −0.3 dBFS, re-encoding **both** from the same wav so Safari and Chrome never hear different levels.

The six CC0 UI sounds replaced in that sign-off are **edits of physical recordings already licensed in this pack** — a real mouse switch, a real toggle switch, a real key, a real door latch, a real sheet of paper. They were rejected for being too synthetic, or for being the Kenney interface pack that already appears in the owner's other games; nothing from Kenney was used to replace them. The freesound page and author of each underlying recording is the row given above, and the trim/pitch applied to it is stated in the row.

These edits do not affect the CC0 licences: CC0 permits modification and redistribution without conditions. They do not launder the proprietary six either — editing Microsoft's or Apple's audio does not make it ours.
